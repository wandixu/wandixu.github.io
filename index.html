About Me

I'm a PhD candidate in computational chemistry at Johns Hopkins University, using molecular dynamics simulations, quantum chemistry calculations and machine leatning to understand how nanoscale structure drives biological and materials function.

My research sites at the intersection of physical chemistry, applying computational and data science skills to real-world challenges in materials discovery, personal care & cosmetics formulation, and R&D consulting. I've contributed to 6 publications and peer-reviewed for Scientific Reports.

Outside the lab, I enjoy reading, paint in watercolor and acrylic, sketch, and stay active through hiking, tennis, badminton, swimming, and snowboarding.

Projects

Selected research projects and contributions.

01

Multiscale Lipid Vesicle Simulations

Built a 12 nm all-atom DPPC vesicle and coarse-grained vesicles (12–60 nm) using MARTINI and DPD. Validated structural and dynamical consistency across scales through bilayer thickness, tail end-to-end distances, density distributions, Van Hove functions, and scattering analyses.

GROMACS MARTINI DPD Python
Paper (in revision) →
02

Protein–Gold Nanoparticle Binding

Performed all-atom MD simulations of MPA-coated gold nanoparticles interacting with cytochrome c, aprotinin, and ubiquitin. Identified key binding residues and analyzed structural responses (RMSD, Rg, secondary structure) over 100–200 ns trajectories.

All-atom MD CHARMM FF NAMD VMD
Paper (in preparation) →
03

Engineering Ligand Nanoparticle with planer lipid Surfaces

Constructing all-atom models of MUA- and MUTAB-functionalized gold nanoparticles using CHARMM parameters. Investigating structural and dynamical properties of ligand surfaces as a function of ligand composition and solution conditions.

GROMACS OPLS Nanoparticles DPD
In Progress →
04

Senolytic Drug Mechanism Studies

Contributed experimental work to three published senolytic drug studies — including flavonoid-based senolytics, ferroptosis-inducing lipid senolytics, and fucoidan senotherapeutics enhancing SIRT6-dependent DNA repair.
